## Have any products pivoted, rebranded, shut down, or launched major new modes since Oct 2026?

### Takeaway
No listed Paperful comparison product appears shut down. ## Are corpus-size, Zotero, LibKey/PDF, and SR-screen claims still accurate?

### Takeaway
**Elicit ~138M and one-way Zotero import** still match. **Consensus corpus is now 400M+** (not a vague “Yes”); LibKey remains **link-resolve**, not full-text ingest; Zotero is still **one-way import**. Edison **175M+** (docs) vs **150M+ figures** (Feb 2026 blog) tension remains — keep Paperful’s “do not reconcile” note. Valency **500M+** and 36 MCP tools match. SciSpace corpus marketing drifted **270M→280M+** across pages; Zotero is import/agent-on-library, not restore. Elicit/SciSpace SR UIs remain **Partial**, not PRISMA-grade Yes. Covidence/Rayyan/DistillerSR still own dual-review screening; AI assist expanded (especially DistillerSR Smart Screening / Rayyan ResearchPilot).
