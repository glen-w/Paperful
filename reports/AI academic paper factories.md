# Fill the library, leave Kosmos hosted

As of October 2026, **Edison Scientific** is the commercial FutureHouse spinout that sells a **hosted, biology-first AI-scientist platform**: Literature (PaperQA3 over a 175M+ paper/trial/patent corpus), Precedent novelty search, Analysis and Molecules lab agents, and **Kosmos**, a credit-gated orchestrator that is **not on the public API** ([docs overview](https://docs.edisonscientific.com/); [Tasks](https://docs.edisonscientific.com/edison-client/tasks.md)). Adjacent “paper factories” are not one market. They split by job: autonomous ML-scientist prototypes, hosted literature search, systematic-review screening, PDF reading, manuscript drafting, and local open-source stacks. **None of them own the full factory** of discover → get PDF → screen → extract → synthesize → novelty → write a journal manuscript → keep current, with local/offline control and a Zotero restore ledger. **Paperful v1 is a disk-first Zotero helper whose public promise is Wanted (missing PDFs)** plus optional Discover and Ask over **held** files; that is the empty cell every hosted product skips (campus proxy, grey playbooks, `snapshot`/`restore`). For `docs/comparison.md`, `docs/comparison-reference.md`, and `docs/ROADMAP.md`, treat Edison as a **complement to route to**, thicken a **factory-stage Yes/Partial/No matrix** rather than a second Edison analogue table, borrow **local PaperQA2-style cited RAG, novelty-vs-synthesis split, honest failure, PRISMA counts, and `stuff`**, and **do not** clone Kosmos world models, a 150M figure corpus, ChemCrow/Molecules, publisher-gray scraping, or manuscript generation that Elsevier’s 2026 author factsheet treats as substituting for human intellectual contribution ([Elsevier factsheet PDF](https://researcheracademy.elsevier.com/uploads/2026-06/genai_factsheet_authors_v1_updated-1.pdf)).

## Platforms cluster by job; nobody ships a full factory

The phrase “academic paper factory” in 2026 marketing covers four different artifacts: an **analyst memo**, a **Wikipedia-like survey**, a **workshop-grade ML manuscript**, and a **hypothesis memo**. Sakana’s own ICLR 2025 workshop experiment produced a fully AI-written paper that scored above that workshop’s average acceptance threshold and was **withdrawn by prior agreement**; Sakana’s internal conference-bar review said **none of the three papers would pass**, and the authors documented a false LSTM citation attributed to Goodfellow ([Sakana first-publication blog](https://sakana.ai/ai-scientist-first-publication/)). Google’s Co-Scientist, rolled toward Labs **Hypothesis Generation** in May 2026, outputs hypotheses and protocols for experimental verification and **does not release source**, citing safety of unmonitored agents ([Nature Co-Scientist](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10644-y); [DeepMind blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/co-scientist-a-multi-agent-ai-partner-to-accelerate-research/)). OpenAI, Gemini, and Perplexity **Deep Research** products write cited longform from the web in minutes; OpenAI’s launch post states the agent can **hallucinate facts** and mishandle citations ([OpenAI Deep Research](https://openai.com/index/introducing-deep-research/)). STORM’s authors say the system is useful in **pre-writing** and **cannot produce publication-ready articles** ([STORM project](https://storm-project.stanford.edu/research/storm/)). GPT Researcher’s authors say it is **not a recommendation for academic papers** ([GPT Researcher README](https://github.com/assafelovic/gpt-researcher)). A strict factory—manuscript plus figures plus real citations plus novelty check plus (for reviews) PRISMA plus code/data plus a submission path—**does not exist as a reliable public product**.

## Borrow traces and a novelty split; refuse the PhD-in-a-day product

Kosmos’s paper is the quantitative source, not a product SLA: up to **12 hours**, shared world model, expert eval **79.4%** of 102 statements accurate, with **interpretation at 57%**—the failure mode that would poison a manuscript factory ([arXiv 2511.02824](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.02824)). Edison’s ACU grant **requires human validation** of Kosmos findings before publication ([ACU Grant](https://edisonscientific.com/news/edison-platform-acu-grant)). An AutoResearch prototype with a four-step bibliographic checker cut invalid citations from **31% to 3.5%** in the authors’ annotation study, which is evidence that **unverified factories routinely hallucinate bibliography** ([arXiv 2607.02520](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02520v1)). Elsevier’s June 2026 author factsheet allows AI to summarize literature and improve language and forbids writing **substantive parts** without the author’s intellectual contribution, fabricating references, and generating primary images; authors must **read each source** ([Elsevier genAI policies](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals); [factsheet](https://researcheracademy.elsevier.com/uploads/2026-06/genai_factsheet_authors_v1_updated-1.pdf)). Springer Nature retracted a machine-learning book after **25 of 46** references were unverifiable ([Retraction Watch](https://retractionwatch.com/2025/08/04/springer-nature-retracts-book-fake-citations-help-us-find-more/)). Commercial writers (Jenni, SciSpace, Overleaf Dimensions, Grammarly Citation Finder) mostly **draft then attach real index records**; that prevents ghost DOIs, not **semantic ghosts** (real paper, wrong claim). Related-work research treats novelty as **positioning this paper**, which AutoSurvey never takes as input ([Li & Ouyang survey](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.11588v1)).

## Keep 1.x as Wanted; call v2 gated packs after the ledger is honest

Paperful already has a **playbook factory** in `docs/research-pack.md` (`refs gap` → ingest → `run` → `dedupe` → optional `summarize`/`ask`) with human gates. `paperful all` is fill+tidy+summarize, **not** a scientist. That is the honest architecture: **factory means a sequence of dry-run verbs**, not Kosmos. Public 1.x copy stays locked: **“find missing PDFs in Zotero.”** Never promise every paywalled PDF. Keep Ask, Discover, and snowball **out of title, meta, share card, and GitHub About**. Sci-Hub remains an opt-in capability in tables and **absent from marketing**. Wanted excellence is the recommended main lane after 1.1.x; stranger evidence decides anything bigger.

Safe **v2 subtitle** (does not replace 1.x): after the Wanted list is honest, grow the collection, keep it current, and ask **your** PDFs. ## Conclusion

The market taught the wrong lesson if Paperful hears “become Kosmos.” The lesson is that **hosted scientists and Deep Research memos outsource the library**, then ask the model to interpret—and interpretation is where Kosmos’s own paper is weakest. Paperful’s scarce asset is the opposite: a **fail-closed path from a Zotero item to a file on disk with a restore ledger**, which is the only substrate on which local PaperQA-style citations, `stuff`, and human novelty paragraphs are scientifically and legally defensible. Build v2 as packs on that substrate; keep pointing life-science discovery, 175M-corpus review, and 12-hour code loops at Edison; keep 1.x’s search phrase exactly where it is.
