paperful

Don’t want to install and run it yourself?

Let me do it for you. Buy me a coffee and I’ll send you your library, all clean and shiny, full of PDFs.

Buy me a coffee

If you will install it yourself, the tool is the open-source page: paperful.app. glenwright.earth/Paperful opens the same page.

What you get back

Your library, in the reference manager you already use. RIS, BibTeX, CSV, or EndNote XML, the PDFs, and misses.csv for anything I could not fetch. Zotero is the one that is well tested. Mendeley and EndNote adapters are seeking testers.

The PDFs are the open-access copies the license lets you keep. You already have somewhere to put them: a zip you will store, or a WebDAV URL you already have. I hand that over. I do not host the library. If the terms do not allow the file, that item stays on the miss list and you get the link.

A miss is one of these: no_doi, paywalled, no_oa, fetch_failed, license_blocked.

I only pull from open-access indexes: Unpaywall, OpenAlex, arXiv, bioRxiv/medRxiv, Europe PMC, Semantic Scholar, CORE, and the item’s own URL. Your campus login stays on your machine.

A full Zotero quota is a normal result. Citation data sync is already free. The quota is attachments: 300 MB free, then paid Zotero storage. WebDAV is for a personal library only. Group libraries cannot use WebDAV. Files on WebDAV are not the same as files in the zotero.org web library. Zotero will not support a third-party WebDAV server.

If a grant line needs a receipt, say so when you buy the coffee.
