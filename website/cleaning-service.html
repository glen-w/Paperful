Don’t want to install and run it yourself?

Let me do it for you. Buy me a coffee and I’ll send you your library, all clean and shiny, full of PDFs.

Buy me a coffee

If you will install it yourself, start on the open-source page or jump to Install.

What you get back

Your library, in the reference manager you already use. RIS, BibTeX, CSV, or EndNote XML, the PDFs, and misses.csv for anything I could not fetch.

The PDFs are the open-access copies the license lets you keep. You already have somewhere to put them: a zip you will store, or a WebDAV URL you already have.

I only pull from open-access indexes: Unpaywall, OpenAlex, arXiv, bioRxiv/medRxiv, Europe PMC, Semantic Scholar, CORE, and the item’s own URL. Your campus login stays on your machine.
